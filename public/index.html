    How to play

    1. Goal: be the first to move all four marbles from your base around the board and into your home row (the coloured lane pointing at the centre).
    2. Getting out: roll a 1 or a 6 to put a marble on your coloured start hole.
    3. Moving: marbles travel clockwise the exact number rolled. Marbles can't jump: if any marble is in the way, that move is blocked. You also can't land on your own marble.
    4. Aggravate! Land exactly on an opponent's marble to send it back to their base.
    5. Sixes: rolling a 6 gives you another turn.
    6. Home: you need the exact count to move into your home row, and you can't pass your own marbles there.
    7. Shortcut through the centre: the four gold stars are the inner corners. Seen from your side they are the 6, 9, 12 and 3 o'clock corners, in the order your marble reaches them; 3 o'clock is just before your home. If your move reaches a corner (where the marble starts, or part-way through the move) with exactly 2 pips left, it can go through the centre (1 pip) to any corner further clockwise (the 2nd pip) — you choose which.
    8. You can't stop in the centre, and you can't hop straight from corner to corner: every shortcut goes through the centre. Shortcuts only go forward, never past your 3 o'clock corner, and any marble in the way blocks them.

    Click a glowing marble, then a glowing hole. Drag to spin the board, scroll/pinch to zoom. Press Space or click the die to roll.
